Origin

The Story Behind the Method

In twenty nineteen, Kenneth Leffer—a data engineer with more than twenty years of experience in statistical analysis—began compiling and organizing the historical results of Powerball drawings dating back to nineteen ninety-two.

Over the course of three years, he mapped more than six thousand drawings and identified frequency patterns in publicly available data that had never been organized into a practical method accessible to everyday lottery players.

The result was a structured approach that allows players to select numbers using real historical data instead of hunches, birthdays, or random Quick Picks.

Today, more than fourteen thousand players across all fifty U.S. states use the method to organize their lottery picks.
