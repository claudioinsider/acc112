Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions.

Who is this product for?
Anyone interested in understanding how everyday food and movement habits influence blood sugar — whether you're newly navigating glycemia concerns, supporting a loved one, or simply curious about building a more balanced lifestyle.

No. Sweet Life Balance is strictly educational and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your physician before making changes to your diet, exercise, or any medical routine.
